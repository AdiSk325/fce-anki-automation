## Part 2 – Open cloze (6 luk)

Wpisz **jedno** słowo w każdą lukę.

*A young athlete's first big race*

Amara has been training (1) ___ the age of twelve, and this summer she was chosen to represent her country at (2) ___ Youth Olympic Games. She is sensitive (3) ___ pressure, so her coach advised her to concentrate (4) ___ her own race, not on her competitors. (5) ___ the end, she finished second, which she had not expected. "Over the years, I (6) ___ learnt that effort matters more than the result," she said.

## Part 4 – Key word transformation (4 zadania)

Uzupełnij drugie zdanie tak, aby znaczyło to samo co pierwsze. Użyj **od dwóch do pięciu słów**, w tym słowa podanego w CAPS. Nie zmieniaj tego słowa.

Po wpisaniu przeczytaj na głos całe drugie zdanie i sprawdź, czy ma czasownik.

1. It was not necessary for the spectators to pay, because the match was free.
   NEEDN'T – The spectators ___ because the match was free.

2. It is a good idea to warm up before you run.
   PAYS – It ___ up before you run.

3. People believe that he is the fastest runner in the club.
   BELIEVED – He ___ the fastest runner in the club.

4. I started playing tennis three years ago and I still play.
   BEEN – I ___ tennis for three years.

## Klucz

**Open cloze**

1. since (*has been training since the age of twelve* – z Present Perfect Continuous pasuje *since*)
2. the (*the Youth Olympic Games* – nazwa wydarzenia)
3. to (*sensitive to*)
4. on (*concentrate on*)
5. In (*In the end*)
6. have (*I have learnt* – Present Perfect przy *over the years*)

**KWT**

1. **needn't have paid** (też: *need not have paid*)
2. **pays to warm** (*It pays to warm up*; musi być czasownik po *to*)
3. **is believed to be**
4. **have been playing** (też: *'ve been playing*)

Co sprawdzić: w 1 *have* + imiesłów (nie samo *paid*), w 2 czasownik *warm*, w 3 *to be*, w 4 Present Perfect Continuous.
