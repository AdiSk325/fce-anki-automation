## Part 2 – Open cloze (6 luk)

Wpisz **jedno** słowo w każdą lukę.

*Learning to play an instrument*

Many people believe that musical ability is something you are born (1) ___, but research suggests that practice matters far more. Children who play (2) ___ least half an hour a day usually make rapid progress. Of course, it helps to be sensitive (3) ___ rhythm and sound, yet even a beginner can improve (4) ___ regular lessons. Teachers often advise students to concentrate (5) ___ small, clear goals rather than on perfection. (6) ___ the years, many learners discover that what they enjoy most is simply playing with other people.

## Part 4 – Key word transformation (4 zadania)

Uzupełnij drugie zdanie tak, aby znaczyło to samo co pierwsze. Użyj **od dwóch do pięciu słów**, w tym słowa podanego w CAPS. Nie zmieniaj tego słowa.

Po wpisaniu przeczytaj na głos całe drugie zdanie i sprawdź, czy ma czasownik.

1. It was not necessary for us to book a table because the restaurant was empty.
   NEED – We ___ a table because the restaurant was empty.

2. People say that she is the best singer in the choir.
   SAID – She ___ the best singer in the choir.

3. I started working in this company in 2022 and I still work here.
   BEEN – I ___ in this company since 2022.

4. We later found out that the concert had not been cancelled.
   TURNED – It ___ the concert had not been cancelled.

## Klucz

**Open cloze**

1. with (*be born with* a talent)
2. for (*play for at least half an hour*)
3. to (*sensitive to*)
4. with (też: *through*) (*improve with regular lessons*)
5. on (*concentrate on*)
6. Over (*Over the years*)

**KWT**

1. **didn't need to book** (też: *did not need to book*)
2. **is said to be**
3. **have been working** (też: *'ve been working*)
4. **turned out** (też: *turned out that*, jeśli zmieścisz w limicie; wystarczy *turned out*)

Co sprawdzić: w 1 i 3 pełny czasownik w drugiej części, w 2 *to be* (nie samo *said*), w 4 Past Perfect jest już w zdaniu, więc luka to tylko *turned out*.
