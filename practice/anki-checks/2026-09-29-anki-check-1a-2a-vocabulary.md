## Part A – Key word transformation

Uzupełnij drugie zdanie tak, żeby znaczyło to samo co pierwsze. Użyj słowa kluczowego bez zmieniania go. Wpisz od 2 do 5 słów, łącznie ze słowem kluczowym.

1. It really annoys me when people are late.
   **NERVES**
   It really ______ when people are late.

2. We need to throw away all these old papers.
   **RID**
   We need to ______ all these old papers.

3. The long winter makes me feel depressed.
   **DOWN**
   The long winter ______.

4. He finds it difficult to explain his ideas clearly to other people.
   **ACROSS**
   He finds it difficult to ______ clearly to other people.

5. She passed all her exams without any problems.
   **THROUGH**
   She ______ without any problems.

6. Please tell me the most important thing immediately.
   **POINT**
   Please ______.

7. Many parents take part in the school's activities.
   **INVOLVED**
   Many parents ______ the school's activities.

8. I'll phone you back immediately.
   **STRAIGHT**
   I'll phone you back ______.

## Part B – Word formation

Uzupełnij luki formą słowa podanego wielkimi literami.

My grandfather was probably the most (9) ______ (INFLUENCE) person in my life. As a young man, he was extremely (10) ______ (AMBITION) and dreamed of running his own business. He was also (11) ______ (PASSION) about music and played the violin every evening. When his first company failed, he showed great (12) ______ (DETERMINE) and started again. He always remained (13) ______ (OPTIMIST), even in difficult times. He was a very (14) ______ (SENSE) man, so people often asked him for practical advice. His friends valued his (15) ______ (LOYAL), because he never let them down. Above all, he taught me to have (16) ______ (CONFIDENT) in myself.
