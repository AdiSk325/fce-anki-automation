# 3A Reading – Famous athletes (notatka źródłowa)

Źródło: zrzuty ekranu z podręcznika Empower Upper-Intermediate, unit 3, ćwiczenie 5 *Reading and Speaking* – trzy teksty o sportowcach (Mike Weir, Chris Froome, Maria Sharapova). Frazy zaznaczone w tekstach na żółto i zielono są wyborem użytkownika; dopisek na marginesie (*packed (with activities) schedule*) też. Zrzutów nie ma w repo (`*.png` jest w `.gitignore`).

## Zaznaczone frazy

**Mike Weir (golf, operacja laserowa oczu)**

| Fraza w tekście | Forma do nauki |
|-----------------|----------------|
| he is **the only** Canadian player **ever to win** a major world championship | the only / first / last + noun + (ever) to + infinitive |
| he **went on to** win a series of top golfing championships | go on to do sth |
| which **gives them a huge competitive advantage over** players with normal eyesight | give sb a (huge) competitive advantage over sb |

**Chris Froome (kolarstwo, astma i salbutamol)**

| Fraza w tekście | Forma do nauki |
|-----------------|----------------|
| **is well known for** his outstanding performance | be well known for sth |
| **he was awarded** a bronze medal | be awarded sth (strona bierna z dwoma dopełnieniami) |
| Froome **suffers from** asthma | suffer from sth |
| **on the advice of** his doctor | on the advice of sb |
| **the case** against him **was dropped** | drop the case against sb |

**Maria Sharapova (tenis, meldonium)**

| Fraza w tekście | Forma do nauki |
|-----------------|----------------|
| has **become a household name** | become a household name |
| her career has **not been free of controversy** | be free of / from sth |
| **was banned from playing** for two years | be banned from doing sth |
| **caused a widespread public outcry** | cause a widespread public outcry |

**Dopisek z marginesu**: *packed (with activities) schedule* → a packed schedule.

## Pytania z ćwiczenia 5b (do sesji speaking)

Jeśli „sport isn't as fair as we like to think", czy zawodnicy powinni móc szukać sposobów na poprawę wyników? Które z tych są akceptowalne i dlaczego: ciężkie treningi, operacja (np. wymiana mięśni ramienia, poprawa wzroku), legalne substancje poprawiające wyniki (np. napoje energetyczne), nielegalne substancje (np. doping).
