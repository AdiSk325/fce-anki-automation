### Poniedziałek – 5 zdań: szyk multi-word verbs (talia 3A)

Wpisz w lukę zaimek (it / them / me) we właściwym miejscu zdania, tak jak w poprawnej wersji. Jeśli zaimek nie pasuje w danym miejscu, wybierz zdanie, które jest poprawne.

Wybierz poprawne zdanie (a lub b):

1. a) I took off them. b) I took them off.
2. a) Please calm down her. b) Please calm her down.
3. a) He gave up smoking last year. b) He gave smoking up it last year.
4. a) My alarm went off at six. b) I went off my alarm at six.
5. a) She broke up with him. b) She broke with him up.

### Środa – 4 zdania PL → EN (Present Perfect i stałe połączenia)

4. Mimo że było późno, wciąż pracowaliśmy.

### Czwartek – 4 KWT (2–5 słów w luce, nie zmieniaj słowa w CAPS)

Po wpisaniu przeczytaj całe drugie zdanie i sprawdź, czy ma czasownik.

1. It is a good idea to learn new words every day.
   PAYS – It ___ new words every day.
2. I started learning the guitar two years ago.
   BEEN – I ___ the guitar for two years.
3. She managed to win the race.
   SUCCEEDED – She ___ the race.
4. It is believed that he is the best player in the team.
   BELIEVED – He ___ the best player in the team.

## Klucz

**Poniedziałek:** 1. b 2. b 3. a 4. a 5. a

**Środa (przykładowe poprawne wersje):**

1. I have lived in this city for three years. (też: I've lived / I've been living)
2. I have a talent for music and I'm grateful to my parents for it. (też: I'm grateful to my parents for that)
3. How long have you been singing in the choir? (też: How long have you sung…)
4. Even though it was late, we were still working. (też: We were still working even though it was late)

**Czwartek:**

1. pays to learn
2. have been learning
3. succeeded in winning
4. is believed to be

Uwaga: w 3. *succeeded in* + -ing (nie *succeeded to*). W 4. po *is believed* następuje bezokolicznik *to be*.
