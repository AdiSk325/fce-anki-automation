### Poniedziałek – 5 luk open cloze (przyimki zależne)

Wpisz **jedno** słowo w każdą lukę.

1. She is proud ___ her sister, who is good ___ chess.
2. I'm looking forward ___ seeing you, but I'm a bit worried ___ the journey.
3. He succeeded ___ finishing the race in spite ___ the pain.
4. We were told to concentrate ___ the exercise and not to depend ___ the teacher.
5. The medal was awarded ___ her for her effort.

### Wtorek – 5 zdań: ułóż szyk (multi-word verbs)

Najpierw sklej czasownik wielowyrazowy w całość.

1. up / came / with / she / idea / an / good
2. it / pick / please / up / can / you / ?
3. on / about / he / goes / always / his / holidays
4. out / of / we / petrol / ran
5. you / the / let / team / down / mustn't

### Środa – 4 zdania PL → EN

Przetłumacz na angielski, bez podglądania kart.

1. Od kilku lat uczę się angielskiego i coraz lepiej go rozumiem.
2. Zawodnik reprezentował kraj na igrzyskach olimpijskich.
3. Poszliśmy autostradą, bo mecz zaczynał się za godzinę.
4. Pokonała rywalkę i wygrała turniej.

### Czwartek – 4 KWT (2–5 słów w luce, nie zmieniaj słowa w CAPS)

Po wpisaniu przeczytaj całe drugie zdanie i sprawdź, czy ma czasownik.

1. It wasn't necessary to buy tickets, because entry was free.
   NEEDN'T – We ___ tickets, because entry was free.
2. People believe that the coach is very strict.
   BELIEVED – The coach ___ very strict.
3. I began working here in 2020 and I still work here.
   BEEN – I ___ here since 2020.
4. She invented a clever answer to the question.
   WITH – She ___ a clever answer to the question.

## Klucz

**Poniedziałek:** 1. of, at 2. to, about 3. in, of 4. on, on 5. to

**Wtorek:**

1. She came up with a good idea.
2. Can you pick it up, please?
3. He always goes on about his holidays.
4. We ran out of petrol.
5. You mustn't let the team down.

**Środa (przykładowe poprawne wersje):**

1. I've been learning English for a few years and I understand it better and better.
2. The player represented his country at the Olympic Games.
3. We went along the motorway because the match started in an hour.
4. She beat her opponent and won the tournament.

**Czwartek:**

1. needn't have bought
2. is believed to be
3. have been working
4. came up with

Uwaga: w 1 *have* + imiesłów, w 2 *to be*, w 3 Present Perfect Continuous, w 4 cząstka *up* zostaje przy czasowniku, a *with* przy cząstce.
